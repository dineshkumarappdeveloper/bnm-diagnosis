Account & Data Deletion

How to delete your BNM Billing account and associated data

This page explains how to request deletion of your BNM Billing account and the data associated with it. It applies to the app published by BNM.

How to request deletion

Option 1 — Remove a counter (immediate, self-service)
In BNM BusinessStudio → Billing → Counters, the business owner can revoke a counter's pairing. This immediately ends that device's session and removes its access.
Option 2 — Delete your account & data (email request)
Email support@bnmapp.com from your registered email address with the subject "Delete my BNM Billing data" and include your business name. We will verify the request and process it.

What gets deleted

  • Your counter pairings and device sessions.
  • Customer details you entered for invoices (names, phone numbers, GSTINs).
  • Your billing records (invoices and line items) associated with the account.

What may be retained

Certain invoice records may be retained where we are legally required to keep them for tax, GST, and accounting compliance. These are kept only for the statutory retention period and then deleted.

Timeline

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days of verification. You will receive a confirmation once the deletion is complete.
