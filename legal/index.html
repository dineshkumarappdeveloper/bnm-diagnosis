BNM Billing

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 June 2026

BNM Billing ("the app", "we") is a billing / point-of-sale app that lets a business and its billing counters create invoices and orders — online or offline — for the business's own BNM account. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and how you can have it deleted.

Who we are

The app is provided by BNM. For any privacy request, contact support@bnmapp.com.

What we collect

  • Sign-in / pairing data — the business owner's email address when signing in directly, or a one-time pairing code / link when a counter device is paired. Each counter is assigned a randomly generated counter identifier (e.g. dev-…) so its offline invoice numbering stays unique.
  • Billing details you enter — to create an invoice you may enter a customer's name, phone number, and GSTIN. These are optional and are entered by the business operator.
  • Sales records — invoices, product line items, quantities, amounts, tax, and payment status you create, which sync to your business's BNM account.

What we do not collect

No location, no contacts, no photos or media, and no advertising identifiers. The app contains no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Why we use it

  • To create, number, and sync your invoices and orders.
  • To keep billing counters paired to the correct business and prevent duplicate invoice numbers.
  • To send an invoice/receipt to a customer when you choose to.

How it's stored & secured

Data is stored in your business's BNM account on our backend (Supabase) and is encrypted in transit over HTTPS. The app is offline-first: invoices are stored locally on the device and synced to the server when a connection is available.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties for advertising. Data is processed only by our infrastructure provider to operate the service, and is accessible only within your own BNM business account.

Retention & deletion

Data is retained while your account/counter is active. You can request deletion at any time — see Account & Data Deletion. Some invoice records may be retained to meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations, after which they are deleted.

Children

The app is a business tool intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
